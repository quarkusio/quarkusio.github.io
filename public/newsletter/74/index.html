Enterprise support options for Quarkus

The Quarkus community just got stronger: alongside the long‑standing Red Hat Build of Quarkus, IBM has announced the IBM Enterprise Build of Quarkus. Organizations now have a choice between two enterprise distributions built on the same community core — a clear sign of the project's growth and maturity.

Get Red Hat Build of Quarkus 3.40, a downstream enterprise-supported version of Quarkus. Read the Red Hat Build of Quarkus 3.40 documentation.

Check out IBM Enterprise Build of Quarkus 3.40 for cloud-native and AI-infused applications. Read the IBM Build of Quarkus 3.40 documentation.

Continued support for 2.16 and 3.20 can be found at HeroDevs.
